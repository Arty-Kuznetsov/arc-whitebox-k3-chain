If corr is high and beta_hat sits near the theoretical slope, the ladder is geometric per
neuron and the step between two cumulant orders predicts the remaining error. k=1 is free,
so a working k2 rung is accuracy for no compute. A working k3 rung would mean k=3 + one
extrapolation reaches k=4-class accuracy without ever forming a 4th-cumulant tensor.
